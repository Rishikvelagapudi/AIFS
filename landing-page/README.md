# Landing Page Module — AIFS

**Fusion AI Summit 2026: AI for Public Good & Civic Governance**

## Overview
The Landing Page serves as the executive presentation portal and entry gateway for the Artificial Intelligence Forensic System (AIFS). It welcomes dignitaries, citizens, and operators with keynote addresses, leadership patronage, and civic AI awareness comic panels.

## Features
- **Hon'ble Patrons & Leadership Header:** Circular gold-framed portraits of executive leadership and patrons.
- **Honorable & Distinguished Industry Speakers:** Left and right flanking galleries highlighting ministers, district magistrates, and tech leaders.
- **Centerpiece Summit Branding:** Cinematic Fusion AI Summit logo with ambient glow and "Use AI Responsibly" mission banner.
- **4 Comic Art Public Awareness Panels:**
  1. *Civic Cyber Police:* Deepfake scam busting & voice clone prevention.
  2. *Smart City Command:* Real-time traffic, emergency ambulance green corridors, and road repair telemetry.
  3. *Transit Safety Desk:* Commuter education against synthetic phishing.
  4. *Civic Town Hall:* Responsible AI ethics and transparent public governance.
- **Interactive Lightbox Modal:** Full-screen zoom and inspection for comic art illustrations.
- **Unified AIFS Navigation Footer:** Direct single-click routing to:
  - **Module 1:** Incident Desk
  - **Module 2:** AI Forensic Laboratory
  - **Module 3:** Post Fetch & Behavior Analysis

## File Structure
- `frontend/src/landing-page/`
  - `LandingPage.jsx`: Main landing page presentation component.
  - `leadersData.js`: Patron and executive leadership data.
  - `speakersData.js`: Keynote and industry speaker profiles.
  - `awarenessData.js`: Comic awareness panel data and action tips.
  - `index.js`: Clean public exports.
